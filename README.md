An entry for the [One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb) bounty on make.fun.

The task: write one sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the
modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of 1800 —
no words coined after their time, no list of features, just a picture
they could hold in their head.

Our sentence (148 characters):

> The internet is a post office that never sleeps, where a letter crosses the world in a blink and returns with whole libraries before the ink is dry.

One familiar institution — the post office — carries the whole
explanation, and it is two-way: a letter goes out, and knowledge comes
back with it. Every word is one a person from 1800 already knows.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes that one sentence
to `/output/entry.txt`.
